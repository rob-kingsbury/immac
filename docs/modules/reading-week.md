# Reading Week

No scheduled classes. Use the time to catch up on unfinished work and to shore up anything that still feels shaky, since the material ahead builds directly on what came before.

A useful way to spend an hour: open your project, pick one page, and rebuild it from scratch without looking at what you already wrote. Whatever you have to look up is what to review.

Check Brightspace for anything due and for what comes next.
